--- EMAIL 2 (Consideration, send 4 days later) ---
Subject: Why your AI proof of concept didn't make it to production

There's a pattern we see repeatedly: a promising AI prototype that never ships. Not because the model wasn't capable — because the infrastructure around it wasn't ready.

The culprits are usually the same: no evaluation framework before deployment, no observability on inputs and outputs, no fallback when the model misbehaves. A model producing impressive demos but failing unpredictably in production is worse than no automation at all. It quietly erodes team trust and creates technical debt that compounds.

At Proficient, our starting point is always reliability, not capability. That means building test sets from real historical data before writing production code, designing human-in-the-loop checkpoints for high-consequence workflows, and making pipelines observable from day one.

The models are rarely the hard part. The engineering around them is.
