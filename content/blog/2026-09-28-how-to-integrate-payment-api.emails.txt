Subject: The part of payment integration that quietly causes the most damage

Declined cards and validation errors are easy to handle. The problems that actually hurt growth-stage teams are subtler: a missing idempotency key that charges a customer twice during a timeout, a webhook handler that doesn't verify signatures and accepts spoofed confirmations, or an event pipeline that processes fulfillment before checking current payment state.

These aren't edge cases. They're the norm in production at scale.

At Proficient, we help technical teams audit and rebuild payment infrastructure before these issues compound. That means reviewing your architecture against the full payment lifecycle — not just the happy path — and identifying where your current setup will break under real load or a determined bad actor.

Most teams are one chargeback spike or compliance review away from a painful rebuild.
